STAGE 3 ESTIMATE (from the measured sizes; the lead decides)
  What a model would have to do, and what it would buy:
    - region slice (670 of 945 missed words, 64 of 605 pure-keep blocks): no model. Five rule fixes (clusters 1, 2, 3, 6, 7), one round, then a new
      hold-out H3 (new scenes in h2_scenes.py: the generator, units, labeller and report exist, so ~20-30 scenes are one small round plus ~10 min of
      Vision reads) to say whether the fixes generalise. The ceiling after the region fixes is word R 0.935 (doc) / 0.955 (vn).
    - inline slice (275 words doc, 271 vn: 6.5 / 4.5 points of word recall): a span judge on the band the rules do not decide (the section 3 word shape).
      Data: H2 already holds 7.0k (doc) and 8.8k (vn) labelled words in 1767 blocks (+ cases, papers); rung-1 boosted trees train in minutes. Rounds: (1) features
      from the word boxes (chip tint, pitch, neighbours) and the training set from H2 + a second generated set, (2) train and measure on H3 against the rules,
      (3) integrate behind an off-by-default flag with a no-regression gate (default path stays safe). About 3 rounds, ~1.5-2 days of wall time with the
      usual review; the cheaper first step is a chip detector as a rule (clusters 5), which covers styled inline code (all of H2's but the bare paths).
    - value check: a kept inline span helps only when the engine is told to leave it (ProtectedSpans) or when it can be drawn from the original pixels
      (the inline-math pixel path, deferred); until then the inline slice costs a mistranslated identifier, not a missing region.
  Recommendation: no model now. Fix the five rules, build H3, re-measure; ask for the model only if the inline slice still matters after that.
