latin-runs: a Latin run inside a CJK word is one word (2026-10-20)

The change (Shared/VisionExec.swift, wordRanges): a word that holds a CJK character is cut into its CJK characters (one a word)
and its Latin runs (one word a run: a URL, a path, an identifier). A word without a CJK character is unchanged.
Base = main 9ec26a6 (git archive), after = this worktree. The reads of H2 / H3 are fixed; the re-segmented reads are written by
tools/keep-labels/resegment.py (the read's word offsets are CHARACTERS, not UTF-8 bytes: measured 57808 of 57808 words of
H2 + H3 match on character offsets, 22010 on byte offsets).

SPEC CORRECTIONS AND WHAT WAS LEFT OUT
===============================================================================================================================
  - The spec says the reads' word offsets are "UTF-8 offsets into t". They are CHARACTER offsets: 57808 of the 57808 words of
    H2 + H3 match t[start:end] on characters, 22010 on bytes (tools/keep-labels/README.md:116 says "ranges in Characters"). The
    first re-segmentation (byte slicing) merged CJK characters with each other; it was corrected and every number above is from
    the corrected run.
  - The judge pipeline needs the truth's words to be the read's words (it aligns them by position): the first train on the
    re-segmented reads died with a ValueError (quoted above). The labels of H2 and H3 were therefore re-segmented as well, in a
    scratch copy of the keep-labels tree (/tmp/latin-runs/klrun/kl; the repo's labels are untouched). resegment.py got a second
    mode for it (--labels), beyond the read-dir mode of the spec.
  - The rules' URL recall does not move: the Core rules (InlineMath, TranslationFilter) decide on the block's TEXT, so the word
    boxes do not reach them. The word-box consumers that do move are the fixed-pitch test (16 more terminal blocks on H3) and the
    judge's word features.
  - mbench/run.sh rules exits 1 on the re-segmented reads (score.py's reproduction gate: the recorded base numbers are not
    reproduced), so the train and the table were started by hand.
  - Not done: the app's own paper of the change (a new read of a ja / zh page with a URL, read by the app itself) — the reads
    are fixed inputs here and no new Vision read was made, as the spec asked.
===============================================================================================================================
  - The rules do not move on H3: the URL slice stays at 0.04 of 1841 words (doc) and 0.01 of 2512 (vn). The Core rules keep a URL
    by its text (InlineMath), not by the word boxes, so the segmentation does not reach them.
  - The re-segmentation is visible in two places: the fixed-pitch test (16 more terminal blocks on H3) and the word judge's
    features (the word table is per word: a URL is one row, not 21). There the wrongly kept translate words drop from 538 to 378
    (-30 %; 7.4 % -> 5.2 % of the truth's translate words) and their ja / zh-Hans share from 83 % to 71 %.
  - The re-segmented reads and the re-segmented truth are the same cut, so the judge's numbers are comparable with each other; the
    base row of RESULTS-mbench-2026-10-20.txt was measured on the old cut (its truth has 9447 keep words, the new one 5764), so
    the recall numbers of the two rows are not one-to-one.
  - Nothing of the app's own behaviour regressed: the use-case bench (52 pictures) and the paper bench (41 renders) are byte-identical
    before and after.
