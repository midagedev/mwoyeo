## Decomposition and timeline, written before the run (prediction: /tmp/own-paper/prediction.txt)

Terms: 41 renders; per render from the b113 run of the old document (page 17.1 s, crop 8.7 s mean) the wall was predicted at
13 x 17.1 + 28 x 8.7 = 466 s, band 300-900 s. Result 185 s: the band was missed on the low side. The b113 times were taken at a higher
machine load; at load 4-6 a page read is 5.7 s and a crop 3.8 s. Cold compiles (one Neural Engine compile per model and app id) were
paid by the warm render, so no scored render carries one.
Per render, the resources: ANE (Vision text read + document read) is the wall; host CPU (erase masks, layout, CoreText, PNG) ~1-2 s
under it; GPU (MI-GAN, 13.3 ms per 512 x 512 tile) negligible; PCIe none; DRAM a few hundred MB; NVMe two PNGs. The renders are one at a
time by the bench's rules (one Neural Engine, and a concurrent render changes the answer), so no asynchrony lever exists across renders.

3-15 -> 3, formula_translated pages 3-6 0-8 -> 11 (miss). The misses are one finding, not noise: the document reader returns one block
the size of the page on pages that mix two columns with a full-width float (p2: core 1349 x 1805 px holding 32 other blocks; p7-c1: 19;
p4-c2: 9; p12-s1: 20), which is what cross_col counts (detail.txt, p2 cross_col lines).
