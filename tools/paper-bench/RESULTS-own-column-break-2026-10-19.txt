## The other 36 renders

  `core / lines / chars / source` of every `block:` line: identical to the base render in the 36 own renders that do not fire (and 45 of 46 of 1706, and
  46 of 46 after the p13 re-run below). The full `block:` lines are NOT byte-identical in 14 of those 36 (p10, p9, p3-c1, ...): they differ in
  `text=` (1-3 px) and `size=` only, which come from the translation of the block. The translation engine is not deterministic ("조건 A" / "조건 a", "MI-Gan" /
  "MI-GAn"), and the base is not identical to itself: two renders of the base binary (base-own and base-own-b: p9, p10, p9-c1, p10-c1, p10-c2) differ in the
  same fields, two renders of the candidate (cand-own, cand-own-b: p1, p1-c1) differ in the same way. The design's "byte-identical block lines" holds for
  the fields that the pipeline decides, not for those the engine decides.
